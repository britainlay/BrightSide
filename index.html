FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers.

Do you come to me?

Yes. BrightSide is a mobile detailing service, so we bring the service to your location within our current service area.

How long does a detail take?

Time varies depending on the package, vehicle size, and vehicle condition.

Can you remove every stain?

No. Some stains are permanent or may have changed the material's color. We aim to improve the condition without promising complete removal.

Do you clean cloth seats?

Yes. Appropriate cloth upholstery can be cleaned and extracted as part of an interior service.

Do you clean SUVs and trucks?

Yes. Larger vehicles may have different starting prices because they generally require more time and product.

What if my vehicle is heavily soiled?

Extremely dirty vehicles, excessive pet hair, heavy buildup, or unusually neglected conditions may require additional work. Any additional charge will be discussed before service begins.

How do I book?

Choose a package, enter your vehicle information, confirm your location, and select an available appointment.
